title: Cormorants' Curious Cooling Conduct
---
Birds, much like other animals, use various means of thermorregulation.
They don't have sweat glands like humans do: instead, most of them open their beak.
This is a common sight during hotter months: still birds with an open beak for no apparent reason.

Nontheless, cormorants use a much more curious method.
They not only open their beak, but they also do a funny thing with their throat to pump air in order not to overheat.
